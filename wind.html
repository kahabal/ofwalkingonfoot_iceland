About the wind

Wind played a very important role throughout the project, as continuous exposure to the element was an integral part of the experience.

After two weeks of walking, a strong wind overturned and damaged my trolley, forcing me to find a way to better understand and protect myself from the wind. I decided to attach a piece of reflective cloth to the front of my backpack, near my shoulder. This allowed me to perceive the direction of the wind and notice sudden changes in its movement.

This simulation is based on entries in my field diary, where I recorded the strongest wind events, combined with data collected from weather stations according to my GPS position.

The result is an attempt to translate these observations into a simulation that conveys something of what it meant to be continuously exposed to the wind.
